# Original-resolution production media — sourcing leads (not included)

The old 1920x1080 scene plates were enlarged from ~716x356-pixel concept GIF frames and were rejected. They have been intentionally excluded from this package. No production photographs have been downloaded or licensed into this folder.

Before using external media, review the image itself, confirm actual source pixel dimensions and commercial reuse terms, download the original-resolution file, and keep credits and any required license notices. Avoid composites containing people, branding, text, or fake UI. Wikimedia pages below were checked for existence and license metadata; they are leads, NOT approved final selections.

Potential sources:

- **Calm ocean texture:** Matt Moloney, 'Ocean Water Background.jpg', 4000x2667, CC0 per Wikimedia page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ocean_Water_Background.jpg
